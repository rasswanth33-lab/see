# Seeable Hub --- Brand & Design System

## 1. Brand Overview

**Brand:** Seeable Hub\
**Category:** Software Company\
**Brand direction:** Modern, approachable, innovative, clean, premium,
and technology-focused.

The visual identity combines a deep navy foundation with blue-to-purple
accents. The overall design should feel sophisticated and digital
without becoming overly futuristic or complicated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Logo System

### Primary Logo

The primary lockup contains:

-   **seeable** wordmark
-   **hub** inside a rounded rectangular outline
-   Pixel/square cluster emerging from the upper-right of the hub mark
-   **SOFTWARE COMPANY** subtitle beneath the main wordmark
-   Thin horizontal accent lines on both sides of the subtitle

### Secondary Logo

Use the stacked version when the horizontal primary logo does not fit:

-   `hub` mark positioned above
-   `seeable` wordmark underneath
-   `SOFTWARE COMPANY` subtitle below
-   Small horizontal accent lines around the subtitle

### Logo Icon / Mark

Two standalone marks are available:

1.  Rounded-square `a` icon
2.  Standalone `hub` outlined mark with pixel cluster

### Logo Rules

-   Always use approved logo artwork.
-   Maintain generous clear space around the logo.
-   Do not stretch, compress, rotate, or distort the logo.
-   Do not change the approved colors.
-   Do not modify the typography or spacing.
-   Prefer the primary logo for official communications.
-   On dark backgrounds, use an approved light/white logo variation
    where available.

## 4. Typography

### Primary Typeface --- Poppins

Use **Poppins** for:

-   Main headings
-   Brand statements
-   Navigation
-   Buttons
-   Marketing titles
-   Display typography

Recommended weights:

-   Regular --- body/supporting text
-   Medium --- navigation and labels
-   SemiBold --- subheadings
-   Bold --- major headings

### Secondary Typeface --- Inter

Use **Inter** for:

-   Body copy
-   Descriptions
-   Forms
-   Technical information
-   UI text
-   Small labels

The combination of Poppins + Inter creates a clean technology-company
appearance while keeping long-form content highly readable.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 5. Visual Language

### Core Characteristics

The design should communicate:

-   Innovation
-   Clarity
-   Connectivity
-   Software expertise
-   Reliability
-   Premium quality
-   Approachability

### Graphic Elements

Reuse the following visual motifs throughout the brand:

-   Small square/pixel clusters
-   Rounded rectangular outlines
-   Thin horizontal lines
-   Blue and purple gradients
-   Generous white space
-   Rounded corners
-   Subtle geometric details

The pixel cluster represents digital connectivity, modular technology,
and a growing software ecosystem.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 6. UI / Website Design Direction

### Overall Style

Create a **premium modern SaaS/software-company interface**.

Recommended characteristics:

-   White or deep-navy base
-   Blue/purple gradient accents
-   Soft glassmorphism where appropriate
-   Large clean typography
-   Rounded cards
-   Subtle shadows
-   Fine borders
-   Smooth hover transitions
-   Spacious layouts
-   Strong visual hierarchy

### Cards

Cards should use:

-   16--24px corner radius
-   Very subtle borders
-   Light shadows
-   Comfortable internal spacing
-   Minimal decoration
-   Pixel motifs only as occasional accents

### Buttons

Primary button:

-   Electric blue or blue-purple gradient
-   White text
-   Rounded corners
-   Medium-to-bold typography

Secondary button:

-   White/transparent background
-   Navy text
-   Thin navy/blue border

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 7. Hero Section

The hero should immediately establish Seeable Hub as a modern software
company.

Recommended structure:

**Headline**\
Clear, confident statement about building smarter digital solutions.

**Supporting text**\
Short explanation of services and value proposition.

**Primary CTA**\
Start a Project / Get Started

**Secondary CTA**\
View Services / Explore Work

Use subtle pixel particles or geometric elements around the hero without
distracting from the headline.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Photography and product visuals should feel:

-   Clean
-   Professional
-   Contemporary
-   High quality

## 9. Merchandise Direction

The brand system can extend to:

-   T-shirts
-   Hoodies
-   Backpacks
-   Mugs
-   Water bottles
-   Notebooks
-   Caps
-   Tote bags

### Merchandise Style

Use the deep navy, white, blue, and purple palette.

Keep the logo placement clean and restrained. Pixel clusters can be used
as small secondary graphics on corners, sleeves, lower sections, or
accessories.

The merchandise should feel like premium technology-company merchandise
rather than promotional advertising.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 10. Spacing & Layout

Use generous spacing to preserve the premium appearance.

Suggested spacing scale:

-   8px --- micro spacing
-   12px --- compact spacing
-   16px --- standard spacing
-   24px --- component spacing
-   32px --- section spacing
-   48px --- large spacing
-   64px+ --- major section separation

Use a strong grid and consistent alignment.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 11. Border Radius

Recommended radius system:

-   Small UI elements: `8px`
-   Buttons: `10–12px`
-   Cards: `16–20px`
-   Large containers: `24px`
-   Hero / feature panels: `24–32px`

The rounded geometry should visually connect with the rounded `hub` logo
outline.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 12. Do / Don't

### Do

-   Keep layouts minimal and spacious.
-   Use navy as the visual foundation.
-   Use blue and purple as controlled accents.
-   Maintain consistent typography.
-   Use pixel graphics sparingly.
-   Keep the logo proportions unchanged.
-   Use gradients for emphasis.
-   Prioritize readability and hierarchy.

### Don't

-   Use random colors outside the brand palette.
-   Overuse gradients.
-   Overuse pixel decorations.
-   Add excessive shadows.
-   Distort the logo.
-   Use multiple unrelated fonts.
-   Make every section visually busy.
-   Use overly futuristic neon effects.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 13. Brand Personality

Seeable Hub should feel:

**Modern + Smart + Reliable + Creative + Premium + Approachable**

The identity should look credible enough for enterprise clients while
remaining energetic enough for startups and technology products.
